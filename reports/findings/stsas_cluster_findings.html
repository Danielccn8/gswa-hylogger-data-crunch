    Every metre is forced into one of the 8 rock types, even when it matches none of them well. These are the metres that fit worst. Each card shows what its rock type normally contains, and what this metre actually contains.

    How to read it Expected is the average make-up of that rock type. Found is what this metre really holds. The multiplier is how far out it sits compared with a normal member of the same rock type — 2.5× means two and a half times further from the centre than typical.
